What's Inside a Disi Bump Cap

A bump cap protects against bumps, scrapes and minor impacts in areas with low-hanging obstacles — with the comfort of a regular cap.

01
Outer fabric

Cotton / polyester with breathable mesh panels.

02
ABS protective shell

Rigid inner shell that spreads impact energy.

03
EVA impact absorber

Foam padding between head and shell.

04
Ventilation & reflective strip

Air holes, mesh and optional reflective detail.

05
Adjustable fastening

Back strap for a secure fit on every head size.
