Industrial Bump Caps from a Turkish Manufacturer

A bump cap combines the look and comfort of a baseball cap with an ABS inner shell and EVA padding that protect the head against bumps and scrapes on fixed objects. It is the right choice where there is no risk of falling objects but low clearances — assembly lines, warehouses, aircraft maintenance and vehicle service pits.

Disi Safety has produced Shelter® and Lymra® bump caps in its 1,000 m² Istanbul facility since 1986. Distributors and corporate buyers order directly from the factory with custom colors, embroidery and private label packaging.

What is the difference between EN 812 and EN 397?

EN 812 covers industrial bump caps against bumps on fixed objects. EN 397 covers safety helmets against falling objects. A bump cap does not replace a helmet on construction sites.
